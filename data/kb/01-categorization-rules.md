# Categorization Rules

## Subscription Rule
A transaction counts as a subscription when it recurs regularly and is billed on a repeating schedule such as monthly or yearly.

## Travel Rule
Travel expenses include flights, hotels, ride shares, and other trip-related purchases.

## Groceries Rule
Groceries include food and household staples bought from supermarkets or grocery stores.

## Dining Rule
Dining includes restaurants, coffee shops, and takeout purchases.
